# Privacy Policy — Page Doodler

Last updated: October 26, 2026

## Data Collection

Page Doodler does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The extension works entirely offline after installation.

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API:
- Doodle strokes (saved per URL)
- Tool preferences (brush size, color, shape type, line type, screenshot mode)

No data ever leaves your browser.

## Third Parties

Page Doodler does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or any third-party services.
